Repo founded 2026-10-26. MoonDeep is the studio's painterly fantasy adventure RPG (Odette and Odile; Battle-Lust, Depth, the conditioning tracks, the Untouched Path). Design was approved the same day it was completed, and the build was commissioned with the engine ruled Rust + Bevy: a pure-Rust core (`moondeep-core`) holding every rule, deterministic and headless-testable, plus a thin Bevy shell (`moondeep-app`) that draws and plays sound but never decides. Two binding conditions ride the commission: the build deliberately uses Bevy tooling new to the studio, as a learning vehicle (roadmap §3); and the build is simultaneously designed as the Moonlight Track (M1–M8) for Hands-On, hosted by Odette with Odile as second voice (roadmap §6). The keeper header (pendulum and swan feathers, borderless) was approved the same day and ships in assets/. The repo carries the design of record in docs/, this bloch memory tree, and the project skill in skills/moondeep/. Next step is S0 (workspace + tuning skeleton); art sequencing is the standing schedule risk and no art date is promised.
