# Hypnosis × Battle-Lust — Design Note for the MoonDeep Game

**Status:** Design proposal by the studio, 2026-10-26, from the studio's direction: leverage
Jessika's Curse's Battle-Lust mechanic with hypnosis, modeled on how *Remothered*
makes hypnosis central — going deeper carries both cost and benefit — adapted to a
fantasy adventure, explicitly **not** horror. Behavior/concept level only; no
Remothered assets, text, or characters are used. All tier values are ours to tune.

---

## 1. What Remothered actually does (sourced)

**The article the studio linked** (*The Power of Hypnosis*, remothered.com,
2026-10-01) describes *Remothered: Red Nun's Legacy*, the trilogy finale
(releasing 2026-10-27, i.e. the day after this note). The protagonist, Susan,
encounters **metronomes throughout the villa that draw her into a hypnotic
state**; per the developers: each session places "her mind at risk, taking a
little more as the trance deepens, yet in return it opens the door to visions."
Powers gained: **Retrocognition** (objects replay their past — buried
conversations, story fragments, puzzle clues), **Secrets in Mirrors** (mirrors
reveal hidden points of interest and elements invisible in the real world,
which then manifest in the environment), **Moth Manipulation** (guiding a
swarm as an active exploration/progression tool).

**The series' long-term effect of hypnosis** (from the released games,
*Tormented Fathers* and *Broken Porcelain*, via the Remothered wiki and
community plot documentation):

- Hypnosis there runs on a **network**: a *leader* controls a *medium*
  ("Mother Acherontia"), who controls the *minions*. Participants are left in
  a **hypnotic loop that recurs from time to time** — conditioning does not
  end when the session ends.
- **Triggers persist**: a song or an object can pull memories (and the trance)
  back years later. Subjects suffer **memory erasure/fragmentation**;
  Rosemary Reed's memories return only near the source and would be lost
  again if she left.
- **Someone else ends up resident in your head**: Wyman stored his own
  memories in session participants to keep his Alzheimer's mind whole; a
  fragment of him persists inside Reed ("part of him still exists within
  Reed"). Phenoxyl, the drug behind it, was built to repress/erase traumatic
  memories and was banned for its side effects.
- **Control can change hands**: the medium Gloria overthrew the leader Wyman —
  that state is literally what "broken porcelain" names. The conditioned
  network is a throne anyone deep enough can sit on.

That is horror's version of the long term: *the loop never stops, your
memories are not fully yours, and another self holds a permanent seat.*

## 2. The adaptation — keep the structures, flip the valence

Jessika's Curse already contains the seed: Jessika is cursed with an alter
ego, **Jessy**, who surfaces at inopportune moments. Remothered's "someone
else in your head" does not need inventing — it needs *promoting to a system*.
(In MoonDeep the pair carries the ruled names **Odette** and **Odile**;
the adaptation below uses the ruled names.)

### Two axes, two clocks

| Axis | Clock | Raised by | Spent/relieved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lust** (Battle-Lust, per the design basis §3) | Per expedition; never decays in the field | Enemy lust-channel attacks | Town services only (church tiers, fountain) |
| **Depth** (conditioning, new) | Campaign-long; a ratchet | Voluntary trance sessions at induction sites; harvested enemy imprinting | Almost nothing — see §4 |

Lust asks *"how long can she stay out?"* Depth asks *"how much of her is she
willing to trade, permanently, for power?"* — the Remothered tension, stated
as a bargain the player signs knowingly, tier by tier.

### Induction points (the metronomes, translated)

Going deeper is always **a deliberate act at a place**, never an accident:
swinging shrine-pendulums, a moonwell, a mesmerist's music box in town,
certain dungeon "stilling points." The cost of each tier is stated before
the session (what it takes, what it gives), in the world's voice — the
article's "taking a little more as the trance deepens" made legible.

### The bargain — benefit side (one per tier, cumulative)

- **Tier 1 — Echoes** (Retrocognition): objects and places replay their
  past — lore, puzzle clues, hidden loot; in this world, also the trysts and
  secrets of the people who were there (quest hooks, blackmail, romance
  intel).
- **Tier 2 — Second Sight** (Mirrors): reflective surfaces reveal what is
  hidden — secret doors, true forms under disguises, and *who in a room is
  conditioned, and how deep*.
- **Tier 3 — Whisper Motes** (Moth Manipulation): a mote swarm that scouts
  ahead, distracts enemies, reaches what hands can't.
- **Tier 4 — The Wielded Bar**: her own lust becomes a resource she can
  spend in combat — Charm/Command (order an enemy), Soothe (convert lust
  into party healing/buffs), Read (an enemy's weakness and next move). The
  meter that taxed her now powers her.
- **Tier 5 — Two Hands on the Wheel**: in the Succumb band she no longer
  fully loses control — the player picks from *Odile's* action menu. The
  deepest surrender returns a strange kind of agency: hers, but Odile's.

### The bargain — cost side (the long-term effect, adapted)

- **Susceptibility compounds.** Each tier raises her lust-gain multiplier
  from trance/hypno-class attacks, and widens what counts as an inducer:
  at depth, words, images, and sounds gain on her that do nothing to a
  shallow girl.
- **A named trigger per tier.** Installed openly, shown to the player. An
  ally who knows it can *snap her out of Succumb* (a genuine rescue tool).
  An enemy who learns it can force a trance-check or a lust spike. Her
  triggers become secrets worth guarding — quests can leak them; intel
  play appears around her own mind.
- **Odile accrues.** Each tier, Odile's presence grows: town dialogue
  swaps, idle behaviors, mirror moments, invitations she accepts that
  Odette wouldn't. At Tiers 4–5 Odile can *initiate* choices — presented
  honestly as Odile's, vetoable by the player at a lust cost. Agency is
  negotiated, never silently taken.
- **Shared-source risk.** Girls conditioned by the same source resonate:
  party synergy when they act together — and a mesmerist holding that
  source's pattern can pull all their strings in the same round. Depth is
  a party-level strategic exposure, not just a personal one.

### Fusion with Battle-Lust (the point of the exercise)

- **Enemy imprinting.** Mesmerist-class enemies attack on the lust channel
  *and* imprint. If she wins a fight in which she spent turns tranced, the
  imprint can be harvested in town: progress toward the next Depth tier at
  a discount. What was done to her becomes tuition — dangerous tuition,
  because an enemy-imprinted tier arrives carrying *their* trigger variant
  until it is cleansed (a church-tier-priced service; the town sells both
  directions of her mind, symmetric with the design basis's church/fountain).
- **Trance stance in combat.** The in-fight form of a session: she stops
  resisting for a turn — lust climbs fast, Trance charge builds, hypnosis
  abilities become available. Surrender as a tactical verb.
- **Succumb at depth.** Tier 5's steered Succumb (above) closes the loop:
  the state Jessika's Curse treats as pure loss becomes, at maximum
  surrender, a second moveset — the fantasy of the design: she went so deep
  that even losing control is something she *does well*.

### Endings (structure, not bad/good)

Depth gates the ending space honestly rather than punishing it:
**shallow** — the curse lifted, she remains wholly herself;
**deep** — she keeps the craft and Odile as a permanent partner-self, a
mesmerist in her own right; **circle** — the whole party shares one deep
conditioning: the strongest synergy, the strongest shared vulnerability.
Different lives, each priced in the open. That is the anti-horror move:
Remothered's long term is *the loop owns you*; ours is *she chose, tier by
tier, what the loop would be allowed to mean*.

### Audio contract (production note)

The studio's torori catalogue already specifies the conditioning kit and
its gaps: the induction voice and whisper/affirmation loops are in-house
production (no cleared library source), and a trigger snap must be the
**identical sample every time** — the deliberate inversion of the reaction
system's shuffle-bag rule. Depth sessions are the natural first consumer.

## 3. Open forks

1. **Who conditions her, canonically?** Recommendation: both lanes — the
   curse's shrines for Odette's main line (solitary, divine, eerie-funny),
   a town Mesmerist operator for the party (a service, priced like the
   church). One axis, two doors.
2. **Reversibility.** Recommendation: near-ratchet — a single major quest
   (the Unraveling) can lower one tier, once, taking the tier's ability
   with it. Repeatability would drain the bargain of weight.
3. **Odile's veto.** Recommendation: Odile-initiated choices are vetoable
   at a lust cost (never free, never impossible) — negotiation, not a
   cutscene stat.

## 4. Relation to the design basis

Extends the design-basis notes §3 (Battle-Lust) without contradicting it: lust rules,
bands, sinks, and the expedition clock are unchanged. Depth is the missing
campaign axis the design basis found *undocumented* in the source game (no
corruption system evidenced) — here it is designed fresh, from Remothered's
structure, which is where the studio directed the borrowing.
